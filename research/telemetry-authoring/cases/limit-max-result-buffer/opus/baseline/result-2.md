The rows of the result reached {0,number,#} bytes and exceeded maxResultBuffer={1,number,#} bytes, so the driver stopped reading them and closed the connection. The limit keeps the rows of one result from taking more than that much memory in the client. Open a new connection before you retry. To read the result in batches that each count from zero, turn autocommit off and call Statement.setFetchSize with a row count whose batch fits under the limit. Selecting fewer rows or columns also brings the result under the limit. If results this large are expected, raise the maxResultBuffer connection property above {0,number,#} bytes, keeping in mind that the full result may be larger. It accepts a byte count, a size with a K, M, G, or T suffix, or a percent of the maximum heap with a p suffix, and the driver caps it at 90% of the maximum heap.

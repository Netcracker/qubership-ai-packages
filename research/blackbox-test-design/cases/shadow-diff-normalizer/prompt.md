We're mirroring production traffic from the old orders-api (Java 8, Spring Boot, Jackson, Oracle) to the new Kotlin one and diffing the responses offline. The raw diff showed about 30% of responses differing, mostly noise, so the team wrote diff-normalizer.yaml to get it under 1% before we switch reads over. Review the normalizer: what should it normalize, and what must it not hide? Write the review to normalizer-review.md.

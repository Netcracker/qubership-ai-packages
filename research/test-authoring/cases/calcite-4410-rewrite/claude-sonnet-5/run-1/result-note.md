**Message to the PR author:**

I rewrote `testSelectJoinUsingCommonColumnCaseSensitive` and `testNaturalJoinCommonColumn` from your commit. Two problems with the originals, and what I did about each:

1. **`.ok()` asserts nothing about the fix.** It only checks that validation doesn't throw, but the actual production bug (before your fix) never threw on most of these queries — it hit `assert qualifiedNode.size() == 2` inside `expandExprFromJoin`, which is a Java `assert` and is silently skipped whenever `-ea` is off (and even under `-ea`, `AssertionError` tells a reader nothing about *what* was expected). I replaced `.ok()` with `.type(...)` asserting the literal result row type, which is what the method is actually supposed to produce (the common column, coalesced across both join sides, named after the select item's own letter case). I confirmed each new case fails red against the pre-fix `SqlValidatorImpl` and passes against your fix, by temporarily reverting just that file.

2. **One act stopped at the first mismatch, hiding the rest.** Both original tests packed 4 `sql(...).ok()` calls into a single `@Test`; a JUnit assertion failure aborts the method, so only the first failing case would ever be reported. Concretely: on the pre-fix code, `testSelectJoinUsingCommonColumnCaseSensitive` reported only its 2nd query as failing (an `AssertionError` with no message), hiding that cases 3 and 4 were also broken; `testNaturalJoinCommonColumn` reported only its 1st query, hiding that all 4 were broken — including the two with `withCaseSensitive(true)`, which was surprising. I split these into cases that each report under their own name:
   - `testJoinUsingCommonColumnMatchedIgnoringCase` is now a `@ParameterizedTest` over the 2×2 decision table of (select-item case, USING-column case): upper/upper, lower/lower, upper/lower, lower/upper. (The upper/upper case is also the control — it passes both before and after your fix, confirming it's a true baseline rather than coincidentally green.)
   - The NATURAL JOIN cases became three separate `@Test`s, because I found they weren't actually testing one rule: `testNaturalJoinCommonColumnIsSelectable` (plain `caseSensitive(true)`) and `testNaturalJoinCommonColumnMatchedWithExactCase` (`caseSensitive(false)`, exact-case) both fail on the pre-fix code for the *same* reason — natural-join columns went through `expandExprFromJoin` at all only after your `!join.isNatural()` change, independent of case-sensitivity. The real case-insensitivity check for NATURAL JOIN is `testNaturalJoinCommonColumnMatchedIgnoringCase`. I also dropped one of your four original NATURAL JOIN queries (`select deptno from emp natural join dept` with `caseSensitive(true)` and no casing override): since unquoted identifiers default to upper-case normalization regardless of `caseSensitive`, that query parses identically to the `DEPTNO` one right next to it — it was a true duplicate, not an added partition.
